Bright Digitals - Video Assets Folder
=======================================
You can place your clean 9:16 vertical video files (.mp4 or .webm) in this directory:
e.g.
  public/videos/reel-1.mp4
  public/videos/reel-2.mp4
  public/videos/restaurant-promo.mp4

And in src/data/siteData.ts:
Set videoUrl to:
  videoUrl: '/videos/reel-1.mp4'

Or you can use any direct hosted video URL from your cloud storage or CDN:
  videoUrl: 'https://your-domain.com/video.mp4'
